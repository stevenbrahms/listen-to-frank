Visiting MoMA with Frank

Is MoMA worth it?

Yeah. Most of the big names in modern art are in this one building. Frank's biased. He's not wrong.

What does Frank tell you?

The stuff you don't usually hear. Not the textbook version. The weird little stories behind the work.

Somebody had to dust Monet's pond. Gallery 515.

A machine built to destroy itself, in MoMA's garden. The piano caught fire. Gallery 405.

Before Jasper Johns painted his flag, he destroyed almost everything he'd made. Gallery 408.

Can you do MoMA in two hours?

Yeah. Go straight up to Floor 5, that's the 1880s to the 1940s: Van Gogh in 501, Picasso in 502, Matisse in 506, Monet in 515. Then down to Floor 4 for Rothko in 403, Pollock in 405 and Warhol in 412.

Frank's got over six hours of stories across the museum, 76 of them on Floor 5 alone. So don't try to hear them all. Play the ones that stop you.

Looking for one work in particular?

Type the title or the artist in the search box. Frank tells you the floor and the gallery number, and his story's right there.

Is there a free audio guide?

Yeah, this one. Free, unofficial, no app, no sign-up. It just runs in your phone's browser. MoMA has its own official audio too. That's theirs. This is Frank.

How does it work?

Find the gallery number by the door. Type it in. Frank's probably got something in that room. Bring headphones.
